# Marketing Proposal: Tobb Technologies – Choose Your Solution Campaign

## 1. Executive Summary

This proposal recommends a package-based marketing campaign called **Choose Your Solution**. The campaign will present selected Tobb Technologies services as clear packages so that potential clients can understand the available options, compare benefits and enquire about a solution that suits their needs and budget.

The campaign will combine a proposed service-package structure with practical promotional content such as posters, social media posts, short videos, tutorials and direct enquiry messages.

The package benefits described in this proposal are recommendations for management consideration. They are not presented as existing Tobb Technologies guarantees or confirmed offers.

## 2. Marketing Opportunity

Tobb Technologies provides a range of IT services, including software development, website development, network engineering, IT consulting, hardware support and related services.

**My observation and proposal:** When a company offers many different services, a potential client may not immediately know which services they need or how those services can work together. Presenting selected services in organised packages could make the options easier to understand and give clients a simpler starting point for an enquiry.

The campaign would also give Tobb a consistent way to explain its services across social media, video content, printed material and direct client communication.

This is a marketing opportunity to test. Tobb should compare the campaign's results with its normal enquiry and sales activity before deciding whether to expand it.

## 3. Proposed Campaign and Package Structure

The campaign name is **Choose Your Solution**. It will introduce the following proposed packages:

### 3.1 Website & Software – Essential

- Website development.
- Software development, based on the client's requirements.
- Proposed two-year update service, subject to an agreed scope and management approval.

### 3.2 Website & Software – Growth

- Website development.
- Software development, based on the client's requirements.
- Proposed two-year update service, subject to agreed terms.
- Promotional support to help market the client's completed website or software to its intended audience. The exact promotional activities and limits must be agreed with the client.

### 3.3 Network Engineering – Essential

- Network engineering services based on the client's requirements.
- Proposed two-year network upgrade benefit, subject to an agreed scope and management approval.

### 3.4 Network Engineering – Complete

- Network engineering.
- Software updates, where applicable.
- IT consulting.
- Hardware maintenance.
- User tutorials or guidance.
- Proposed three-year upgrade benefit, subject to agreed terms and service limits.

### 3.5 Zonke – All-in-One Package

This proposed package combines the services listed in the packages above into one broader solution under a proposed five-year service agreement.

The final list of included services, pricing, support arrangements, service limits, renewal conditions and exclusions must be defined by Tobb management before the package is offered. The name **Zonke** should also be reviewed because Tobb has an existing Zonke-related platform or project, and the package name should not confuse clients.

### Important package conditions

The two-year, three-year and five-year benefits are proposals, not confirmed company offers. Before advertising them, management should:

- Calculate the cost and confirm that the business can deliver each benefit.
- Define what counts as an update, an upgrade, maintenance and promotional support.
- Set fair-use limits, response times, exclusions and any additional charges.
- Confirm package prices, payment terms, contract duration and cancellation conditions.
- Approve all discounts, specials and promotional wording.

The campaign should not promise unlimited free work or guaranteed marketing results.

## 4. Target Audience

The campaign will focus on:

- Small and medium-sized businesses that need websites or software.
- Organisations that need network engineering and IT support.
- Entrepreneurs who want to establish or improve their online presence.
- Businesses that need several IT services from one provider.
- Existing clients who may need additional services or ongoing support.

## 5. Benefits to Clients and Tobb Technologies

### Benefits to clients

- Easier-to-understand service options.
- A clearer way to compare package levels.
- The ability to enquire about a solution that matches their needs.
- Access to related services through one provider, where included in the agreed package.
- Clearer expectations about support, updates and service terms.

### Benefits to Tobb Technologies

- A more organised way to present its services.
- Promotional material that can be reused across different channels.
- More opportunities to generate enquiries and request quotations.
- Opportunities to introduce additional services to existing clients.
- A campaign that can be measured and improved using actual results.

## 6. Campaign Promotion and Implementation

### Phase 1: Management review

Management reviews the package structure, service capacity, costs, pricing, contract terms and proposed benefits. The company approves the final package names and advertising claims.

### Phase 2: Create marketing material

Prepare a coordinated set of practical promotional materials:

- A main campaign poster introducing **Choose Your Solution**.
- Individual package posters showing the services and approved benefits.
- A package-comparison graphic.
- Short TikTok and Facebook videos explaining the packages.
- YouTube videos or tutorials explaining Tobb's services and showing how technology can help businesses.
- WhatsApp messages and digital flyers that sales representatives can share with potential clients.

### Phase 3: Promote through suitable channels

Use Tobb's approved communication channels, including:

- TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.
- YouTube for tutorials, demonstrations and company introductions.
- WhatsApp for direct enquiries and sharing approved promotional material.
- The company website and relevant blog content.
- Radio, podcasts, newspapers, flyers and posters where the cost and audience make sense.
- Relevant business, school and community events where Tobb can introduce its services.

The campaign can also feature completed work, staff explanations and client testimonials, but only with the relevant client's permission.

### Phase 4: Manage enquiries

Every campaign advert should include a clear call to action, such as **Contact Tobb for a consultation or quotation**. Enquiries should be recorded so the team can identify which channel and package generated interest.

### Phase 5: Run a pilot and improve

Start with a limited campaign period. Review the enquiries and sales results, collect feedback from potential clients and staff, and improve the package descriptions or promotional content where necessary.

## 7. Measuring Success

Tobb should record a baseline before the campaign begins and compare it with results during and after the pilot.

Suggested measures include:

- Number of enquiries received.
- Number of quotation requests.
- Number of consultations or meetings booked.
- Number of new clients and completed sales.
- Which package receives the most interest.
- Which promotional channel generates the most useful enquiries.
- Social media reach, video views, shares, comments and link clicks.
- Revenue generated and campaign costs.

The company should use actual results rather than assuming that views or likes automatically lead to sales.

## 8. Risks and Considerations

- **Cost of long-term benefits:** Updates, upgrades and maintenance over multiple years may create ongoing costs. Tobb must calculate and limit these commitments before advertising.
- **Unclear package scope:** Each package needs a written list of included services, exclusions and support conditions.
- **Marketing expectations:** Promotional support for a client's website or software must state exactly what Tobb will do. It should not guarantee sales or a specific number of customers.
- **Name confusion:** The proposed Zonke package name needs management review because of the existing Zonke-related platform or project.
- **Client privacy:** Obtain permission before publishing client names, logos, project details, photographs or testimonials.
- **Discounts and specials:** Management must approve prices and promotional periods before publication.

## 9. Conclusion

The **Choose Your Solution** campaign aims to make Tobb Technologies' services easier to understand and promote. By presenting selected services in clear package options and supporting them with consistent digital and traditional marketing, Tobb can test a practical way to attract enquiries and introduce more clients to its services.

The recommended next step is for management to review and cost the proposed packages, approve the final terms, and then run a small campaign pilot. The results should guide any wider rollout.
