# Tobb Technologies – Choose Your Solution Campaign

## Project overview

This project proposes a package-based marketing campaign for Tobb Technologies. The campaign encourages potential clients to choose a service package that matches their needs and budget, instead of seeing Tobb's services only as separate offerings.

The central idea is to make Tobb's services easier to understand, compare and enquire about by presenting clear package options with additional benefits.

## Marketing opportunity

Tobb Technologies provides a range of IT services, including website and software development, network engineering, hosting, hardware-related services, IT consulting, SEO, design and training.

A potential client may not know which services they need or what additional support could help them get value from a technology solution. A clear package offer could make the buying decision easier and give Tobb an opportunity to introduce complementary services.

This is a proposed marketing opportunity based on the assignment brief and the information reviewed about Tobb's services. The proposed packages and benefits are not presented as existing Tobb offers.

## Campaign purpose

To help Tobb attract potential clients by introducing easy-to-understand service packages, promoting special offers and explaining the value clients can receive when they choose Tobb.

## Proposed package concept

### 1. Website and Software – Essential

- Website or software development based on the client's agreed requirements.
- A proposed two-year update benefit, subject to management approval and clearly defined terms.

### 2. Website and Software – Growth

- Website or software development based on the client's agreed requirements.
- A proposed two-year update benefit, subject to management approval and clearly defined terms.
- Marketing and promotional support to introduce the completed website or software to its intended audience. The exact promotional services would be agreed with the client.

### 3. Network Engineering – Essential

- Network engineering services based on the client's requirements.
- A proposed two-year network upgrade benefit, subject to management approval and clearly defined terms.

### 4. Network Engineering – Complete

- Network engineering.
- Software updates where applicable.
- IT consulting.
- Hardware maintenance.
- User tutorials or guidance.
- A proposed three-year network upgrade benefit, subject to management approval and clearly defined terms.

### 5. Zonke – All-in-One Package (proposed name)

A proposed all-in-one package combining the Tobb services discussed in this project into one client agreement, with a proposed five-year term.

The exact services, limits, exclusions, support arrangements, pricing and renewal conditions must be assessed and approved by Tobb before the package is offered. The name must also be checked internally because Tobb has a separate Zonke product/platform.

## Target audience

The campaign is intended for:

- Small and medium-sized businesses that need websites, software or IT support.
- Organisations that need network engineering, hardware support or technology improvements.
- Entrepreneurs who want to establish or improve their online presence.
- Businesses that need help introducing a new website or software solution to customers.
- Potential clients who prefer comparing service options before requesting a quotation.

## Business value to Tobb

If approved and implemented responsibly, the campaign could:

- Make Tobb's services easier for potential clients to understand.
- Encourage enquiries by giving clients clear package choices.
- Create opportunities to combine related services.
- Help Tobb promote additional services such as training, support, hosting and marketing.
- Create opportunities for repeat business through properly scoped maintenance or support agreements.
- Give Tobb clear campaign results to measure, such as enquiries, quotations and sales.

## How Tobb could use the campaign

Tobb could promote the packages through its approved digital and offline channels, including Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, radio, events, flyers and posters.

The campaign could use:

- Package comparison posters.
- Short videos explaining each package.
- Demonstrations and tutorials.
- Client testimonials, with permission.
- Promotional offers with clear terms and closing dates.
- Enquiry links or contact details so interested clients can request a quotation.

A small pilot campaign could be run first. Tobb could record enquiries, quotation requests and confirmed sales to identify which packages and promotional channels attract the most interest.

## Important implementation note

All free update, upgrade, marketing, maintenance and five-year package benefits in this project are proposals. Tobb must confirm its technical capacity, costs, service limits, pricing, contract terms and management approval before advertising or selling them. The campaign must not promise unlimited work or guaranteed business results.
